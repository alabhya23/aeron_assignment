Limitations

Rules use broad screening limits, not instrument calibration specifications. GPS records occur less often than weather readings; their 160 void statuses do not imply 160 station failures. Conflicting S06 rows remain unresolved, and exact duplicate handling assumes matching channel values are redundant. Unflagged measurements are not guaranteed accurate.
